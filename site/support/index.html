Molt support

Questions, problems or privacy requests: write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

Where do deleted photos go?

iPhone and iPad: deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Until then you can get them back there. The space is freed only after you empty Recently Deleted, or after the 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, the photo is also deleted from iCloud and from your other devices.

Android: photos are deleted from the phone right away. They do not go to a trash, so they cannot be restored on the phone. Copies already backed up to Google Photos stay there; other cloud apps may differ. The app cannot see or change them. The space on the phone is freed at once.

Does the app delete anything by itself?

No. Nothing is deleted until you confirm the system's own dialog.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app (or the Pro screen) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions (https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions (https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Molt keeps its data on your device only, so uninstalling the app deletes its cache, statistics, widget thumbnails and settings. Your photos stay in your library unless you deleted them with the app. Purchase records at RevenueCat can be deleted on request; see section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Molt is a photo-library cleanup app for Android and iOS. This website sets no cookies, runs no scripts and makes no requests to other sites.
