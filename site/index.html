How it works

Three steps to a lighter gallery

  1. Scan

    Molt goes through your photo library right on your phone.

  2. Swipe

    Left sends a photo to the bin, right keeps it. A counter shows how much space you are about to free.

  3. Delete

    Review the bin, restore anything, then delete everything at once with one system confirmation.

What it finds

What Molt finds

  • Duplicates

    Exact copies and nearly identical photos.

  • Series

    Runs of almost the same shot.

  • Blurry photos

    Out-of-focus shots you will not miss.

  • Old screenshots

    Screenshots older than 30 days.

One photo in every group of similar photos always stays: your favourite or the sharpest. Molt never offers it for deletion.

Privacy first

Your photos never leave your phone.

  • Analysed on your device

    Photos, thumbnails and results are never uploaded. Analysis works offline.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking

    Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play and by RevenueCat, which manages purchases. They receive purchase data only, never your photos.

  • You decide

    Nothing is deleted until you confirm it in the system dialog.
