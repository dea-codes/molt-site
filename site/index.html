Molt: Photo Cleaner

Clean up your photo library, privately.

Molt finds duplicates, blurry photos and old screenshots in your photo library, and lets you swipe them away. Everything is analysed on your device: there is no backend, no analytics and no tracking, and your photos never leave your phone. Free for the first 250 deletions, then 25 a day; Molt Pro removes the limit.
