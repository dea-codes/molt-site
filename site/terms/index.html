2. What the app does

Molt analyses the photos and videos in your library on your device and suggests items that are likely worth deleting, such as duplicates, series of similar shots, blurry photos and old screenshots. The analysis runs entirely on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

3. Free use and Molt Pro

The app is free for the first 250 deletions. After that you can delete 25 items a day for free. Molt Pro removes this limit. It is offered as a weekly or a yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The price and the billing period are shown in Google Play before you buy.

On Android, confirmed items are deleted from the phone right away. They do not go to a trash and cannot be restored on the phone. Copies already backed up elsewhere, for example in Google Photos, stay there and the app cannot see or change them. Please keep a backup of photos that matter to you.

The suggestions (duplicates, blurry photos, screenshots) are produced by automatic analysis and can be wrong.
